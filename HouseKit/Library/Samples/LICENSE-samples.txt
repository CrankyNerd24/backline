The recorded drum samples in this folder (outside rusty/ and swirly/) are derived from Virtuosity Drums by Versilian Studios,
recorded at Virtuosity Musical Instruments, Boston, MA, performed by Austin McMahon.
https://github.com/sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums

They are released under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication):

No credit is required to use them, or anything made with them.

The samples in rusty/ are derived from Big Rusty Drums by Karoryfer Samples,
and those in swirly/ from Swirly Drums by Karoryfer Samples.
https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.big-rusty-drums
https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.swirly-drums

Both are also released under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication):

No credit is required to use them, or anything made with them.
