Thanks for testing HouseKit, a drummer for guitarists' practice on iPad.

START HERE
The tour runs on first launch (about 3 minutes). It covers almost everything below. To replay it: tap the song name at the top, then Take the Tour.

PLEASE TRY
1. Build a song. Use the starter song, or New Song ▸ Build It for Me… (pick a style, vibe and length). Tap a section to edit it; double-tap one to play from there.
2. Shape it. Set each section's Energy (Chill to Big), change the groove (about 100 in 12 styles), add or reorder sections. Undo and Redo sit left of the tempo.
3. Tell HouseKit. Type or tap a suggestion: "more swing in the chorus", "punchier", "add 4 bars to chorus 2", "faster". Hold the mic to say it instead.
4. Sounds Like. New Song ▸ Sounds Like…: search one of the 70 songs on the list (try "Superstition" or "Back in Black"), describe a feel ("a slow 6/8 blues"), or analyze an audio file you own. Then Hear it, Build the song, or Save the groove.
5. Play along. Tap Play-along mode and play guitar over a song for a few minutes. Try Next section, Fill now, loop, and the countdown near the end of each section. If you have a Bluetooth page turner or MIDI foot controller, pair it in Pedal Setup.
6. Kits. Tap the kit name to open the Kit Editor. Switch between the 12 kits, pick sounds per drum, turn the knobs. Turn on Advanced controls for Tune, Attack and Decay.
7. Export. Export ▸ Save to Files (or Share…) and open the WAV in another app.

WHAT WE MOST WANT TO HEAR
- Any crash, freeze, or moment the drums stop, stutter or crackle. Note what you'd just tapped.
- Timing: do the drums ever drift or feel late, especially with a pedal?
- Sounds Like with your own recordings: was the tempo right? Was the feel (straight or shuffle) right?
- Anything that's cut off, overlapping or hard to read on your iPad model.
- Requests to Tell HouseKit that it didn't understand. Send us the exact words.
- Which kits and grooves you'd use, and which you wouldn't.

KNOWN LIMITS
- iPad only, landscape, iPadOS 17 or later.
- Sounds Like knows 70 songs. Other songs are built from the words you type, or from a recording you analyze.
- Analyzing needs a file the Files app can read, such as an MP3, M4A, WAV or AIFF you own. Apple Music downloads can't be read.
- Voice uses Apple's speech recognition. On iPads that can't recognize speech on the device, it needs an internet connection.
- The share sheet can take a moment to appear for long songs. Save to Files is quicker.
