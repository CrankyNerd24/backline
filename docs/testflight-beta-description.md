HouseKit is a drummer for your practice, built for guitarists and other non-drummers. Lay out your song in sections, say how each part should feel, and play along with real recorded drums on your iPad.

Build a song your way. Start from the starter song, one of 14 templates, or Build It for Me (pick a style, a vibe and a length). Each section gets its own groove from 99 hand-written ones in 12 styles, in 4/4, 3/4, 6/8 or 12/8, and one Energy control from Chill to Big. Count-ins, fills into each chorus and a proper ending come with it.

Start from a song you love. Sounds Like knows the drums of 70 well-known songs, builds from a description like "a slow 6/8 blues", or listens to an audio file you own and measures its tempo, feel and time.

Just say what you want. Type or speak requests like "more swing in the chorus", "punchier" or "add 4 bars to chorus 2". HouseKit tells you what it changed, and Undo takes it back.

Play along. A large Play-along screen you can read from across the room, a countdown to each next section, changes that wait for the bar line, and support for Bluetooth page turners and MIDI foot controllers so your hands stay on the guitar.

Real drums. Three recorded acoustic kits and 12 kits in all, each drum adjustable in the Kit Editor. Export your song as a 24-bit WAV. Every sound is synthesized in the app or public domain, so what you make is yours to use with no license or credit.

Free to try, with four styles, two kits and three songs. One purchase, HouseKit Full, unlocks everything: no subscription, no account and no generative AI. HouseKit collects no data, and your songs stay on your iPad.

Requires an iPad with iPadOS 17 or later, used in landscape.
