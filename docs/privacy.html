HouseKit Privacy Policy

HouseKit collects no data. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your songs and settings stay on your iPad.

Effective 23 October 2026 · applies to HouseKit for iPad, version 1.0 and later

What we collect

Nothing. HouseKit has no servers of its own, so it doesn't send your songs, recordings, requests, usage or device details to us or to anyone else. It doesn't use third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code, and it doesn't share data with data brokers.

What stays on your iPad

HouseKit saves what you make so it's there next time. All of it is stored on your iPad, inside HouseKit:

  • your songs, your own grooves and your own kits;
  • settings such as Show Advanced Controls, your foot-pedal setup and whether you've seen the tour;
  • audio you export, until you save or share it somewhere else.

Like other apps' files, these can be included in your iPad's own iCloud or computer backups, which Apple's terms cover. Deleting HouseKit deletes everything it stored, so save any exports you want to keep first.

Features that use your iPad's hardware

HouseKit asks for permission before using any of these, and you can turn each one off any time in Settings › HouseKit.

Crash reports and TestFlight

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPad's settings, Apple may pass us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. When you test a beta through TestFlight, Apple shares the feedback and screenshots you send, along with the email address you joined with. We use these only to fix problems in HouseKit.

Children

Because HouseKit collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If HouseKit ever starts handling data differently, we'll update this page and its effective date before that version is released.
