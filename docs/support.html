HouseKit Support

Help with HouseKit, the drummer for guitarists' practice on iPad. Most answers are below. If yours isn't, email us and we'll get back to you.

Contact us

info@crankynerdcustoms.com

To help us help you quickly, include your iPad model, your iPadOS version (Settings › General › About), HouseKit's version, and what you were doing when the problem happened. A screenshot or screen recording helps a lot.

Getting started

HouseKit runs on iPad with iPadOS 17 or later, in landscape. The guided tour starts the first time you open it and shows every main feature in about three minutes. To see it again, tap your song's name at the top of the screen and choose Take the Tour.

Common questions

I can't hear the drums

Check the iPad's volume and that it isn't connected to Bluetooth speakers or headphones you didn't mean to use (Control Center shows where audio is going). In Play-along mode, check the Drums level in the side panel.

If HouseKit shows "Couldn't start audio", close it fully (swipe it away in the app switcher) and open it again. Another app holding the audio, such as a call, can cause this.

The drums crackle or stutter

On older iPads, close other apps you aren't using and avoid typing while a song plays. If you're running HouseKit from Xcode or in the Simulator, audio there is less reliable than the installed app.

Voice requests don't work

Go to Settings › HouseKit and turn on Microphone and Speech Recognition. Hold the mic button the whole time you speak, then let go.

Some iPads, including older models, can't recognize speech on the device and need an internet connection for voice. You can always type the same request instead.

HouseKit didn't understand my request

Try the shorter form shown in the suggestions, such as "more swing", "punchier", "add a bridge" or "add 4 bars to chorus 2". Email us the exact words that didn't work and we'll teach HouseKit to understand them.

Sounds Like can't find my song

HouseKit knows 70 well-known songs; tap Songs HouseKit knows to see them. For any other song, describe the feel ("a slow 6/8 blues", "fast country shuffle at 140"), or choose Analyze a recording and pick an audio file of the song.

A recording won't analyze

The file has to be one the Files app can open, such as an MP3, M4A, WAV or AIFF you own, in iCloud Drive or On My iPad. Songs downloaded from Apple Music and other streaming services are copy-protected, so they can't be read. Use at least ten seconds of music with a clear beat.

Connecting a foot pedal

Pair a Bluetooth page turner in the iPad's Settings › Bluetooth, or connect a MIDI foot controller by Bluetooth or USB. Then open Pedal Setup from the pedal button at the top of the screen and press each pedal when asked.

Exporting a song

Tap Export, wait for the WAV to finish, then tap Save to Files and choose a folder, or Share… for AirDrop, Mail and other apps. Every sound in HouseKit is synthesized in the app or public domain, so exports are yours to use anywhere, with no license or credit needed.

Backing up or moving my songs

Songs are saved on your iPad and included in its iCloud or computer backup. Restoring that backup to a new iPad brings them back. Deleting HouseKit deletes its songs, so export any you want to keep first.

Undoing a change

Use Undo and Redo, left of the tempo at the top of the screen. They go back up to 50 changes in the current song.

Refunds

Apple handles purchases, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Testing a beta?

Thank you. Take a screenshot in HouseKit and tap Share Beta Feedback, or open the TestFlight app and tap Send Beta Feedback. Tell us your iPad model and what you were doing.
