# Changelog

HouseKit's versions, newest first. The version is the app's marketing
version (`MARKETING_VERSION`); raise the build number
(`CURRENT_PROJECT_VERSION`) for every upload to TestFlight or the App Store.

## 1.0 — 2026-10-23

The first release: a drummer for guitarists' practice on iPad. Everything
runs on the iPad, with no subscription, no account and no data collected.

### Free and HouseKit Full
- Free to download, with song building, WAV export, Energy, Tell HouseKit,
  Play-along, foot pedals and the tour; the Rock, Blues & Shuffle, Pop and Ballad styles;
  the Garage Kit and Big Rusty; and up to 3 songs.
- **HouseKit Full**, one in-app purchase (no subscription, Family Sharing),
  adds every style, groove and kit, choosing sounds and saving kits in the Kit
  Editor, unlimited songs, the Pattern Editor, and every Sounds
  Like song with Analyze a recording and Save the groove.
- Every groove and Sounds Like song can be heard before buying, and nothing
  already in a song is locked. **Restore Purchases** is in the song menu.

### Songs
- Build a song in sections (intro, verse, chorus, bridge, solo, outro), each
  as many bars as you like, with count-ins, fills into the next section and a
  big ending.
- 14 ready-made song templates, an empty song, and **Build It for Me**: a whole
  song from a style, a vibe, a tempo and a length.
- **Sounds Like**: drums after a real song. Pick from 70 well-known songs,
  describe a feel ("a slow 6/8 blues"), or analyze a recording you own; the
  analysis measures its tempo, straight or shuffle feel, and 3 or 4 beats, then
  picks HouseKit's closest groove.
- 99 hand-written grooves in 12 styles, in 4/4, 3/4, 6/8 and 12/8, with a
  Groove Picker (style tabs, search, live preview) and a Pattern Editor.
- One **Energy** control per section (Chill to Big), plus swing; the busy,
  intensity and human sliders, time feel and cymbals are under Show Advanced
  Controls.
- Double-tap a section to play from it. **Undo** and **Redo** for every change.

### Tell HouseKit
- Plain-language requests, typed or spoken: "more swing in the chorus",
  "punchier", "add a bridge after the second chorus", "add 4 bars to chorus 2",
  "next section". Each reply says what changed, with one-tap Undo.
- Hold-to-talk voice with Apple's speech recognition; the drums duck while you
  speak. On iPads with Apple Intelligence (iPadOS 26), Apple's on-device model
  helps with loosely worded requests.

### Play along
- A large Play-along screen readable from across the room: section, bar and
  beat, the song map, and a countdown to the next section.
- Next and previous section, restart, fill now, big ending, loop, tap tempo and
  practice speed (50–120%); changes land after the bar or at the end of the
  section, so the beat never breaks.
- Bluetooth page turners and MIDI foot controllers, with Pedal Setup.

### Sound
- Three recorded acoustic kits, all public domain (CC0): Virtuosity Drums (a
  club jazz kit), Big Rusty Drums (a big rock kit) and Swirly Drums (a dry
  vintage kit), with several hit strengths and alternate takes per drum.
- 12 built-in kits, including an electronic one. The Kit Editor sets each
  drum's sound, tune, attack, decay, tone, character, room and volume, and
  saves your own kits.
- A sample-accurate engine with room and punch; recordings stay in memory so
  older iPads don't drop out.

### Export
- The whole song as a 48 kHz, 24-bit stereo WAV, matching playback exactly.
  Save to Files, or share. Every sound is synthesized in the app or public
  domain, so what you make is yours to use with no license or credit.

### Getting started
- A 28-step guided tour on the real screens, replayable from the song menu.
- iPad only, landscape, iPadOS 17 or later.
